Stash now follows your language. The Mac app, the Chrome extension and this website are available in 20 languages.

## What changed

- **The Mac app** uses the language of your Mac. Menus, settings, search, capture, the reader and the menu bar panel are translated.
- **The Chrome extension** follows the language of your browser. The popup, the right-click menu, the Save button on X and the bookmark import are translated.
- **The website** has a page for each language. Pick yours at the bottom of the [home page](/).

## The languages

English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語, 한국어, ไทย, Español, Português (Brasil), Français, Italiano, Deutsch, Русский, Українська, Polski, Nederlands, Türkçe, العربية, हिन्दी, Bahasa Indonesia and Tiếng Việt.

<img src="/blog/2026-11-11-stash-in-20-languages/tr.webp" alt="The Stash home page in Turkish" width="1200" height="600" loading="lazy" />

## What stays in English

Blog posts, the privacy policy and the terms. A few macOS texts also stay English for now: the Services menu item and the permission prompts. The name Stash never changes.

See a wrong word? [Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/mrtcmn/stash/issues).
